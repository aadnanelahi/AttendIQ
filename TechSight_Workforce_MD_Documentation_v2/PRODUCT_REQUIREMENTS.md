# Product Requirements

## 1. Organization
The system shall support organizations, legal entities, branches, departments, locations, employees, managers, administrators, and role-based access.

## 2. Attendance
Support biometric/device transactions, manual attendance, mobile attendance, schedules, shifts, rosters, late/early rules, absence, overtime, holidays, corrections, and attendance calculation.

## 3. Leave
Support configurable leave types, balances, accrual rules, applications, approvals, cancellations, attachments, and reporting.

## 4. Payroll
Support payroll processing using approved attendance, overtime, leave, deductions, allowances, employee salary structures, payroll periods, payslips, exports, and integration-ready interfaces. Country-specific payroll/WPS rules must be modular.

## 5. Devices
Support multiple biometric vendors through adapters. Manage device registration, configuration, connectivity, transactions, users, attendance records, synchronization, status, and diagnostics.

## 6. Access Control
Support doors, controllers/devices, access groups, schedules, permissions, events, and access reporting.

## 7. Visitor Management
Support visitor registration, host, visit purpose, scheduled visits, check-in/out, badges, approvals, and visitor history.

## 8. Mobile
Android and iOS application supporting attendance, GPS, geofencing, leave, overtime, manual corrections, approvals, announcements, notifications, and employee self-service.

## 9. Notifications
Email and WhatsApp notification channels with templates, events, preferences, retries, delivery logs, and tenant configuration.

## 10. Reporting
Dashboard, attendance, absence, lateness, overtime, leave, payroll, device, access, visitor, and audit reports. Export to common business formats.

## 11. AI
AI assistant, workforce analytics, anomaly detection, natural-language reporting, attendance insights, overtime analysis, and forecasting. AI must obey tenant and role permissions.

## 12. Integration
REST APIs, webhooks, HR/ERP/payroll integrations, import/export, and vendor device adapters.

## 13. Deployment
Support cloud SaaS, on-premise, and hybrid deployments.
