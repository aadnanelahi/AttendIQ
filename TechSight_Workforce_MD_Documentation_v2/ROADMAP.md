# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation
Architecture, repository, CI/CD, database, identity, tenancy, observability, security baseline.

## Phase 1 — Core Workforce
Organizations, branches, departments, employees, roles, devices, attendance ingestion, attendance calculation.

## Phase 2 — Workforce Management
Shifts, rosters, leave, overtime, approvals, mobile attendance.

## Phase 3 — Payroll
Salary structures, payroll engine, payslips, payroll approval, exports/integration.

## Phase 4 — Access and Visitors
Access control and visitor management.

## Phase 5 — Integrations
HR, ERP, payroll, webhooks, additional device vendors.

## Phase 6 — AI
Natural-language assistant, anomaly detection, insights, forecasting.

## Phase 7 — Enterprise
Advanced HA, enterprise audit, advanced compliance, billing, partner ecosystem.
