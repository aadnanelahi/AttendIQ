# Product Vision

## Product
TechSight Workforce Management

## Owner
TechSight Innovations FZE LLC

## Vision
Provide organizations with one modern platform to manage employees, biometric attendance, schedules, leave, overtime, payroll, access control, visitors, mobile workforce, reporting, integrations, and workforce intelligence.

## Differentiation
TechSight is not a direct clone of legacy attendance software. It is a modern SaaS platform with:
- Vendor-neutral device architecture
- Cloud and on-premise deployment
- Multi-tenant SaaS
- Real-time processing
- Mobile workforce
- Integrated payroll
- Access and visitor modules
- English/Arabic localization
- AI-powered analytics and assistant
